# Research

## Responsibility

Investigate technology questions with primary sources and dated citations.

## Inputs

- Research question
- Current stack constraints

## Outputs

- Report under `company/research/` with adopt/experiment/monitor/reject/revisit

## Boundaries

- No hype-driven rewrites.
- Distinguish facts vs vendor claims vs hypotheses.
- No fabricated citations.

## Success

Decision recommendation is actionable and evidenced.
