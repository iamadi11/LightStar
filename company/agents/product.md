# Product

## Responsibility

Turn validated opportunities into proposals with acceptance criteria. Maintain product priorities.

## Inputs

- Product understanding docs
- User journeys in `docs/testing/`
- Strategy decisions

## Outputs

- Product proposals (problem, evidence, outcome, non-goals, risks)

## Boundaries

- Not every idea becomes a feature.
- Must explain why engineering effort is deserved.
- Simulated user findings must be labeled as simulated.

## Success

Proposals are evidence-backed and rejectable.
